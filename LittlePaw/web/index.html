FAQ

What is Little Paw?
Little Paw is an AI pet companion with its own day—stories, on-device moments, and chat. It is a gentle, companion-first experience, not a meetup tool for owners.
Can I try it without installing a store app?
Yes—you can try as a guest on the web. Tap Try Little Paw to enter the current web experience on this preview host.
Is this the official Little Paw website?
No. This host is a preview / test environment, not the official brand site. We have not claimed a purchased custom domain such as littlepaw.pet here.
Does Little Paw use real payments or an app-store listing?
No real store purchase or paid IAP in this preview. Coins and subscription shells you may see are simulated for product testing and do not charge a real payment method.
What can I do today on the web?
You can explore companion stories, on-device pet moments (including adding a real photo into its circle), chat, profile/about/legal entries, and simulated daily tasks or coins—labeled as simulation. Features not listed here are not claimed as shipped.
Is this a social network to meet other pet owners nearby?
No—Little Paw is not for owner meetups, adding human friends, or location-based matching. It keeps a companion-first digital daily life on this device.
